🎳 BowlMath

A strike is worth 10 plus your next two balls, which is why nobody can score a game by hand after frame 7. BowlMath scores any game from the frame string, tells you the best finish still on the table, and works out your league handicap without the napkin.

  • Full game scoring - paste the frames (X, 7/, 9-, and a 10th like X9/), get the total, the mark count, and the frame-by-frame grid. 300 for twelve strikes, 150 for all fives-and-spares, guaranteed.
  • Max possible - mid-game, the best score still reachable if every remaining ball is a strike. Watch it drop with every open frame, in real time.
  • League handicap - the classic 90% of 220, floored, never negative, plus series totals with handicap baked in.
  • Honest bands - what your average actually says about you, from "the gutter is a feature" to "the house shot knows you."
